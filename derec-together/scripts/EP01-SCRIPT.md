That's exactly 100 characters. Only "actually" and "and" are dropped. Every other word is spoken, in the same order, by 0:16, so whoever clicked hears their title straight away. **[HOST GATE]**

## 5. Hands On (7:10–10:10): tabletop model

**Why a tabletop model, not real software.** It's the pilot, and most viewers are cold. Some of them are listening on the podcast feed, not watching (FORMAT §3). A ruler on paper survives audio if every move is said out loud, and it can't crash. The real-software demo, with the `lib-derec` version on screen, gets its own episode when Facundo has a build he's happy to show **[HOST GATE]**. The IOU card already promises it.

**The analogy, and why it survives scrutiny.** Two dots fix a straight line. One dot lets it swing anywhere. Hide the secret where the line meets the edge of the page, and give each person one dot. Then one dot tells you nothing, and any two tell you everything. For a threshold of two, that isn't a picture of Shamir's scheme. It is the scheme, drawn with ordinary numbers. The Curve takes it to three. Bruce then raises the objection every sharp sceptic raises, and Facundo answers it on camera with the Curve and R6's three limits. That leaves nothing for a commenter to catch.

**The kit.** Build it once and reuse it for every tabletop Hands On.
- **The board.** A2 card in Paper `#F7F3E6`, landscape. Down the left edge, an Ink scale of 100 pages over the full height, with a tick per page and a label every 10. Four card slots across it, centred 12, 24, 36 and 48 cm from the edge and numbered 1–4 in Jade.
- **Four plain Paper cards,** numbered on their feet to match the slots. They're separate cards, not strips cut from one sheet, because a sheet cut into pieces is the jigsaw picture the brand kit bans.
- **Four amber glass tokens,** 12 mm, flat-backed, each with a centre dot. Removable putty holds them on their cards.
- **A clear acrylic ruler,** 50 cm, with a pivot pin at its zero end.
- **Bruce's own cookbook,** with his recipe card tucked in at page 47 **[HOST GATE]**.
- **Four crew hands,** one from each edge of camera D. Never a face, and they never touch each other. They are the four people.
- **Rehearse every pair of cards** before recording. The spin has to stop within about 20° of level for all four tokens to land on their cards. If it stops steeper, Facundo spins again.

**The Curve page (R6).** A new graphic for the brand kit to add.
- Full-frame Paper, framed to match camera D so the dissolve reads as the same page. The same edge scale, simplified: ticks every 10, labels at 0, 50 and 100.
- The kicker **THE CLASSROOM PICTURE**, in S2 Atkinson 600 caps, Jade, where the cards put theirs (x 240, baseline y 216). It labels the drawing honestly, the way LABEL labels the table.
- A 3 px Ink curve and 14 px Ink dots. No gold, no glow, no thread, and no amber, which belongs to props. No axes except the edge, and no grid.
- **Every frame of the wobble is a real curve through the dots on screen.** With one dot it swings through many curves. With two it sways only through the curves that fit both, and its end sweeps the whole edge scale. It never makes a shape no such curve could make.
- At the third dot it settles into one curve over 6 frames on `settle`: no bounce, no overshoot. The settled curve is a gentle arch, never a rising line, so it can't read as a growth chart. "47" sets beside the edge in T4 Fraunces, Ink.
- **S01** as each dot lands: identical every time, panned to the dot, in a gap in the talk. **S03** in the 1.5 s hold at the settle. Nothing under words.
- In and out on a 12-frame cross-dissolve (`breath`). The Leaf belongs to chapter cards.
