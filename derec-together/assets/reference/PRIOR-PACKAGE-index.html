Context, with perspective

Timely questions.
Lasting understanding.

Policy headlines can help explain why people are paying attention. The show remains focused on recovery, privacy, helper networks and practical choices. Each recording gets a fresh check of its time-sensitive claims.

People before panic.

Use clear examples and useful next steps. Avoid fear, investment advice and promises of absolute safety.

Explain the limits.

Recovery does not undo every hack, prevent every scam, or replace careful device and account security.

Make participation approachable.

Invite questions, share readable sources, and give newcomers a way into the community.
