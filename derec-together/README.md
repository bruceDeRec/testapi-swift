# Many Hands

**The DeRec Alliance community podcast.** A YouTube series hosted by Bruce Longley and Facundo La Rocca.

The flagship runs 12–18 minutes and premieres every other Wednesday. A weekly *Light Work* Short goes out every Friday. Every episode opens with the same five-second cold open: Tuck the sea otter is off doing something different each time, and every time it ends with the same small ritual. It's three plain stones that mean nothing alone, and they light up together when enough friends bring theirs.

> *Bruce:* "Many hands…"  *Facundo:* "…make light work."

## Start here

1. **`index.html`** is the review page. Open it in a browser. It has a playable animatic of the Episode 01 cold open with a synthesized sketch of the theme, and every piece of the series on one page.
2. **`intro/COLD-OPEN-STORYBOARD.md`** is the storyboard for the first introduction, frame by frame.
3. **`DECISIONS.md`** holds the creative rulings, and why each call went the way it did.
